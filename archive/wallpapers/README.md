# Old wallpapers

The photographs that used to sit behind the top of every page: Madrid for
Spanish, Paris for French, Rome for Italian, at night in the dark theme and by
day in the light one (webp, with a jpg fallback).

They were retired in the Duolingo-style redesign, which uses flat drawings
instead (`public/assets/art/`, generated by `scripts/draw-art.py`). They are
kept here, outside `public/`, so they are saved but never served or loaded.
